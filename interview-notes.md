## Response Timeframes & Priority Tiers
* **Emergency (Highest Priority):**
  * **Scope:** Water leaks, fire safety, and broken locks.
  * **Target:** 24/7 coverage with response within 2 hours.
* **Urgent:**
  * **Scope:** Non-functioning heat and plumbing.
  * **Target:** Solved within 24 hours.
* **Normal:**
  * **Scope:** Mostly cosmetic issues.
  * **Target:** Solved within 5 working days.

> *"Response doesn't mean it's fixed, means it was seen."*
