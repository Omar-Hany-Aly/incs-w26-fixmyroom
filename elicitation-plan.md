# Elicitation Plan: FixMyRoom

## Stakeholders

| Role | Representative | Interest | Influence | Available | Technique | Justification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Head of housing operations, project client and budget owner | Ms. Sabine Kraus | Pay for project requirements | H | Twice a Week, By Appointment, Available immediately at emergency | Short focused interview | Because of her limited availability: she has high influence but is free only twice a week, by appointment, so a short interview uses her time on key decisions like budget and scope. |
| Head caretaker | Mr. Dariusz Nowak | Leads Caretakers | M | Part timer, Important Meetings | Prototyping, scenarios | Because of tacit knowledge: he knows how urgency is really decided, which is hard to explain in words, so concrete scenarios help him show it. He is part-time, so sessions stay short. |
| Carry out repairs in the buildings | Caretakers | Receives salary, cleaning | L | 5 days in a week, poor at lunch | Observation | Because of tacit knowledge: their daily habits, such as how they choose what to fix first, are things they would not think to mention in an interview. |
| Housing office, tenancy and costs | Ms. Hanna Vogel | Helps tenants in room problems, Process working Money | H | Available 6 days, not available on Sundays | Brainstorming, analogies | Because of risk: complaint handling and cost tracking have many hidden steps. Brainstorming surfaces problems we would not know to ask about. She is available 6 days, so sessions are easy to schedule. |
| Chair of the residents' council | Mr. Kofi Mensah | Speaks on behalf of all residents | M | Important meetings, readily available for tenants | Individual interviews only | Because of risk: he speaks on behalf of all residents. In a group, others could influence his answers, so we interview him alone. |
| Plumbers, electricians and other trades | External contractors | Fix utilities Problems | L | Good, twice a week | Observation, apprenticing | Because of tacit knowledge: trade skills and access problems (basements, plant rooms) are best learned by watching the work in person. |
| Receives reports, approves budgets | Campus Living management board | Analyzes Reports, Distribute Budgets | H | Daily | Separate interviews only | Because of risk: in a group, members may hold back or defer to the most senior person. Separate interviews give us each member's own view. |
| Report problems in their rooms and in shared areas | Residents | Pay Bills, Living | M | Daily | Questionnaire | Because of size: there are several hundred residents, many of them international. We cannot interview everyone, so a questionnaire reaches all of them. |

## Risks

| ID | Risk | Impact | Mitigation / fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Group we cannot reach:** the Campus Living management board is "separate interviews only" and will not be in our 20-minute interview. | **H** | Ask Ms. Kraus which numbers the board needs (BG4), and ask for an existing report as a sample. |
| R2 | **Fact only one person knows:** only Mr. Nowak knows how urgency is really decided, and only Ms. Kraus knows the real budget. | **H** | Cross-check with Ms. Vogel, observe the caretakers, and put these questions in the written follow-up. |
| R3 | **Conflict:** Mr. Nowak and Ms. Vogel may disagree about what counts as urgent. | **M** | Ask both the same question and compare answers. Raise any difference with Ms. Kraus, since she decides. |
| R4 | **Time:** the interview is only 20 minutes but there are six topic areas. | **M** | Prioritize the questions, split them between the interviewers, and send the written follow-up the same day. |
| R5 | **Unrepresentative voice:** Mr. Mensah speaks for residents but may not reflect quiet or international residents. | **M** | Use the questionnaire for all residents, offered in more than one language. |

## Assumptions

| ID | Assumption | If it is wrong |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The availability times in our table are our own estimates, because the brief gives no schedules. | Meetings or observation may need to be rescheduled. |
| A2 | Caretakers and contractors will allow observation, including in basements and plant rooms. | Fall back to interviews or scenarios with Mr. Nowak. |
| A3 | The TA playing the client can speak for the housing office and the board. | Send the unanswered questions in the written follow-up. |
| A4 | Residents will answer a questionnaire in a language they understand. | Response will be low, so use the council chair to encourage replies. |
